**Status: defunct — no operating website, developer portal, or public API.**

24/7 Real Media was a New York digital advertising technology and media company formed when 24/7 Media merged with Real Media in 2001, acquiring the Open AdStream publisher ad server. It was acquired by WPP Group in July 2007 for approximately $649 million ($11.75 per share) and run inside WPP Digital.

The business was renamed 24/7 Media, then merged into WPP's programmatic arm Xaxis in January 2014, retiring the 24/7 brand. Open AdStream was rebranded Xaxis for Publishers and sold to AppNexus later that year. Xaxis itself was sunset into GroupM Nexus in January 2024.

Backed by: trinity-ventures, zero-stage-capital
